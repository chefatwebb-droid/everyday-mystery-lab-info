Application use

Everyday Mystery Lab Upload Bridge is a personal publishing application for the channel owner. It is not offered as a public upload service and has no subscription, payment or viewer sign-in feature.

The owner authorizes uploads and schedules for Everyday Mystery Lab and remains responsible for reviewing educational accuracy, original content, audience settings and applicable YouTube rules. The tool records upload identifiers and stops on unresolved upload state rather than blindly creating duplicates.

Hosting, authorization, API quotas and YouTube processing can interrupt operation. A configured job is not a guarantee of publication, revenue or uninterrupted availability. Existing scheduled videos are released by YouTube according to their saved settings.
